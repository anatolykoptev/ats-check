# Truth and voice rules

The rules every Offersmith skill shares. A resume, a letter and a form answer are all cut from the master resume, and none of them may say more than the master does.

## No fabrication: cite or drop

Every factual claim (a number, a date, a company, a tool, a scope, a team size, a result) traces to a master bullet id, a metric row, or a file you can open. A claim with no source is dropped, or turned into a question for the candidate. Never fill a gap with a plausible guess: a number with no provenance is a future retraction, because a recruiter can check your public footprint.

## One place for each number

A number is written in full in one home bullet of the master, and carries a row in the Metrics bank (value, window, source, strength, home). Everywhere else it is copied from there. A number that changes is changed once.

## Say how the work was done, once

If AI coding agents wrote the code under your review, say so once per document, plainly, then lead bullets with what you designed or built. Do not hide the method and do not repeat it for effect. The same goes for team size and ownership: "led" only where you had reports, "built" where you wrote or directed the build.

## Gaps

A requirement you do not meet is not written into the resume. In a letter, a gap the posting itself names gets one plain sentence ("I have not done this at a developer-tool company.") followed by the nearest real evidence. No apology, no "despite", no second gap. A gap the posting does not raise stays out.

## Voice

Machine-written text has a small set of tells: praise of yourself (exceptional, world-class), filler affirmations, stacked triads, em-dashes as list delimiters, closers like "I look forward to hearing from you", and every bullet in the same verb-noun-metric shape. Fight them with true specifics and uneven sentence length. The checkable part is [kill-list.txt](kill-list.txt); the cover-letter checker enforces it and the resume checker enforces the dash and first-person rules. A last read aloud is still yours: if a sentence could appear in any candidate's letter, replace it with a fact only you could write.

## Confidential names

Some names stay out of every document: a client under NDA, a former employer you may not name, a project you do not want tied to your name. List them in the claim guard under "Never mention in a letter"; the letter checker rejects them. Immigration and work-authorization status are answered only in the form field that asks, in your own words, never inferred and never placed in a resume or letter by a tool.
